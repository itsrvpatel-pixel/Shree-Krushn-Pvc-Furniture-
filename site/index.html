The material

Why PVC, not plywood

Kitchen platforms, washbasin cabinets and monsoon damp are what finish wooden furniture early.

01

100% Waterproof

Our PVC material doesn’t get affected in any weather - hot, rainy, or cold.

02

Termite & Borer Proof - For Life

There’s no organic (wood) material in it, so there’s zero risk of termites, borers, or any insects.

03

Fire Resistant (Self-Extinguishing)

Our PVC has a self-extinguishing property when exposed to fire - unlike ordinary wood, it doesn’t easily catch fire, making it a safer choice for your home

04

Fast installation just in 10 days

Full home pvc furniture installation time is just 10 day

05

Very Low Maintenance

No daily cleaning hassle - just wipe with a damp cloth and it’s shining again. No special polish, oil, or treatment needed.

06

Cost-Effective Premium Appearance

Gives the visual appeal of premium materials (like real wood veneer) at a significantly lower cost, without sacrificing durability.
