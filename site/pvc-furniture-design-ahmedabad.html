Choosing a finish

What actually decides how it looks in a year

Matte over gloss in a kitchen

Gloss shows every fingerprint and every wipe mark. Matte hides both and does not dull with cleaning.

Light body, dark accent

An all-dark kitchen in a standard Ahmedabad flat makes the room feel half its size. One accent run is usually enough.

Grain direction

A wood-grain laminate run the wrong way across two adjacent shutters is the commonest finishing mistake, and it cannot be fixed afterwards.

Handleless costs less than it looks

A J-profile or a routed groove removes the handle line entirely, and in PVC it is a cut rather than an extra part.

Common questions

What people ask us

Which colour combination looks best in PVC furniture?

The one that survives the room, not the one that looks best in the shop. A dark matte finish shows every fingerprint in a kitchen, and a high-gloss white shows every scratch in a childrens room. What works in most Ahmedabad flats is a light body with one darker accent - shutters in a wood-grain laminate against a plain carcass, or a dark base with light overheads so the kitchen does not close in. We bring laminate samples to the site visit and hold them against your actual wall and light, which tells you more in two minutes than an hour of scrolling.

Can PVC furniture be made in any design?

Nearly. It routs, grooves and takes a profile shutter well, and it can be louvred or fluted. Where it differs from plywood is carving and very thin decorative sections - those are better in another material, and we will say so. Anything built to a measurement, which is most of a home, is no harder in PVC than in wood.

Do you have PVC furniture photos of real work?

Over 500, sorted by room, in the app - kitchens, wardrobes, TV units, mandirs, dressing tables, partitions. They are our own jobs in Ahmedabad homes, photographed after handover. You can browse them without registering and save the ones you like, and the ones you save come up at the measuring visit so nobody has to describe a design from memory.

What is the latest design trend in PVC furniture?

Handleless shutters with a J-profile or a groove, fluted panels on a TV wall, and matte finishes instead of high gloss. In kitchens, tall units instead of a loft you need a stool for. These are all straightforward in PVC - the sheet takes a profile well - so a modern look does not cost what it used to.
