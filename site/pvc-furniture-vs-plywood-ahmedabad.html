PVC Furniture vs Plywood

The honest comparison, for the rooms where it actually matters. What the sheet is made of, what it does over ten years, and the question everyone asks - do screws hold in it.

The short answer

Where plywood loses and PVC does not

Kitchen platforms, washbasin cabinets and monsoon damp are what finish wooden furniture early. Plywood is layers of wood held together with glue. Water gets into the edges, the layers lift, and the bottom shelf under a sink is usually the first to go. Termites treat it as food.

A PVC sheet has no wood in it at all. There is nothing for water to swell and nothing for a termite to eat. That is the whole of the difference, and everything below follows from it.

What it is

Hollow sheet, not solid plastic

A honeycomb inside

The sheet is not a solid slab. Inside it runs a honeycomb of ribs and hollow chambers, which is what keeps the weight down without giving up strength.

Lighter than wood, and still strong

Considerably lighter than traditional wood furniture, which makes fitting easier and puts less load on the wall - while still carrying a full wardrobe of clothes or a cupboard of utensils.

Screws and hinges hold

The usual worry about a hollow sheet. Those internal ribs are engineered for it: screws, hinges and handles hold under full load, and soft-close hardware stays where it was fitted.

The industry has already moved

Hollow PVC sheet is now the most widely used material for modular kitchens and wardrobes, because that balance of weight against strength is hard to beat.

Living with it

The things you notice after a year

100% waterproof

Hot, rainy or cold, it does not swell, warp or delaminate. Water near the sink or the stove every day does nothing to it. This is why it belongs in a kitchen and a bathroom before anywhere else.

Termite and borer proof, for life

No organic material means no risk of termites, borers or any insect. Not treated against them - simply nothing there for them.

Fire resistant

The sheet is self-extinguishing. Unlike ordinary wood it does not easily catch fire, which matters most in the room where you cook.

Wipe and done

A damp cloth and it is clean. No polish, no oil, no yearly treatment.

Hygienic surface

The surface is non-porous, so it does not trap bacteria, mould or dust mites the way an open wood grain can. Again, kitchens and bathrooms first.

Cooler to the touch

Those hollow chambers insulate. In Ahmedabad summer the surface does not heat up the way a solid material does.

Quieter doors

The same hollow structure dampens sound a little, so a cabinet door closes with less noise than solid wood or solid PVC.

How it looks

The finish is the laminate, not the sheet

The sheet is the structure. What you see is the laminate on it, in 1mm and 1.25mm - Crystal, Orian, Hexa, Flexibond, Rama and Lionia. Wood textures in teak, walnut and oak, solid colours, and modern finishes. That is where the design choice actually lives, and it is also the main thing that moves the price.

It gives the look of premium material at a lower cost, without the durability going with it. What it costs sets out the rates and what changes them.

And the trees

Nothing is cut for it

No trees are felled to make this furniture, and PVC is recyclable. If that matters to you, it is a straightforward reason to choose it over plywood.

Common questions

What people ask us

Is PVC actually stronger than plywood?

Not in every way, and it would be dishonest to say so. Good marine plywood is stiffer across a long unsupported span. What PVC does is never swell, never rot and never feed termites, and in an Ahmedabad home it is water and termites that end furniture, not a load test. For the long spans we build in hollow PVC, which has ribs inside the sheet.

Does PVC furniture look cheaper than plywood?

The sheet is not what you see - the laminate is, and it is the same laminate used over plywood. We work in 1mm and 1.25mm from Crystal, Orian, Hexa, Flexibond, Rama and Lionia. Put the two side by side with the same laminate and most people cannot tell which is which.

Is PVC more expensive than plywood?

In the same finish and the same hardware they land close to each other. The difference shows later: plywood in a kitchen or under a basin needs attention within a few years, and PVC in those places does not.

Can PVC take the same hardware as plywood?

Yes - the same hinges, the same soft-close tandem channels, the same sliding systems. Nothing about the hardware choice changes because of the sheet.
