Privacy Policy

Shree Krushn PVC Furniture · Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Shree Krushn PVC Furniture app collects, how it's used, and how it's protected. This app is used to manage furniture appointments, design estimates, and project tracking between Shree Krushn PVC Furniture and its customers.

Information We Collect

When you use this app as a customer, we collect:

  • Contact details: your name and phone number, used to identify your account and to contact you about your project.
  • Address details: your address and flat/unit number, used to schedule and locate site visits.
  • Project information: your furniture requirements, measurements, design preferences, and photos of your space, used to prepare estimates and track your project.
  • Photos: reference photos you choose to upload (of your space or design preferences), and progress photos our team uploads during your project.
  • Payment records: amounts and dates of payments made toward your project, used for billing and record-keeping. We do not collect or store card, UPI, or bank account details through this app.
  • Reviews: if you choose to leave a review, your name, rating, and review text may be shown to other customers as a testimonial.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments and site visits
  • Prepare and share design estimates
  • Track the progress of your furniture project
  • Process and record payments toward your project
  • Contact you about your project via phone, WhatsApp, or in-app notifications
  • Show your review (if you choose to leave one and it's featured) to other customers as a testimonial

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Your information is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage), a cloud data platform operated by Google. The app itself is hosted on Vercel. Both providers maintain their own security and privacy standards for the infrastructure they operate.

Who Can Access Your Information

Your project information is accessible to authorized staff of Shree Krushn PVC Furniture who need it to manage your appointment, estimate, or project. It is not shared with unrelated third parties.

Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics to understand general, aggregated app usage (such as which pages are visited) to help us improve the app. This does not identify you personally.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our services and maintain business records. If your customer record is deleted, most associated project data is removed as well; any review you've given that has been featured as a testimonial may be retained separately for marketing purposes even after your record is deleted.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to request deletion of your data, subject to any records we're required to keep for business or legal purposes.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for use by adults arranging furniture services. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
